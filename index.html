This year, Launch Week and The Work College Supervisor Training become one shared, campus-wide experience: "Many Roles, One Goal: Educating Students Together" Professional Development Week. Because education at Berea doesn't happen in just one place. It happens in classrooms, offices, studios, shops, labs, and worksites. It happens through teaching, mentoring, scholarship, and work-learning.

"Many Roles, One Goal: Educating Students Together" is our opportunity to step back, see the whole picture, and strengthen how we support student growth, belonging, and success—together.

We'll focus on four connected areas:
Teaching. Scholarship. Mentoring. Workplace Learning.
Whether you're new to Berea or have been here for years, this week is for you.

Let's begin the year strong—together.
